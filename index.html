Privacy Policy

Personal Translator for monday.com · Effective 11 October 2026 · Published by Babken Grigoryan

Personal Translator for monday.com (“the extension”) shows the names of monday.com boards, groups, columns, status labels and workspaces in the language you choose. It does this by changing the text displayed in your own browser. It does not change the data stored in monday.com. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by monday.com Ltd.

In short: the extension has no server of its own. It sends nothing to its developer, contains no analytics, tracking or advertising, and does not sell or share your data. It contacts other services only when you use a feature that needs them, and each one is described below.

What the extension reads

On pages at monday.com, the extension reads the text that monday displays so it can replace German interface terms with your chosen language. This happens inside your browser.

To help your team complete its dictionary, the extension also keeps a list of short untranslated terms it sees in board, group and column headers and in status labels. It deliberately skips menus, pickers and people columns, so that names and item data are not collected. However, a short piece of text that sits where a label usually sits can still end up in the list. That list stays in your browser unless you choose to send it somewhere, as described below.

What is stored on your computer

The extension stores the following in your browser’s local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This storage is kept on your device and is not synchronised to your Google account.

  • Your settings, such as the display language and whether translation is on.
  • The translation dictionary, and the list of untranslated terms described above.
  • Your name, if you enter it. It is attached to translation suggestions you choose to send.
  • The translation suggestions you have made, and the answers to them.
  • A monday.com API token, only if you are an administrator and connect the extension to a monday board. It is stored unencrypted in local extension storage, and the extension says so when you enter it. “Disconnect token” removes it. A token you enter for a one-off bulk term import is used once and is not stored.
  • An OpenAI API key, only if you use “Translate with AI”. It is remembered after first use so that you do not have to enter it again. The “API key…” button in the translation editor’s settings removes it.

What leaves your computer, and when

RecipientWhenWhat is sent
The web address of your team’s shared dictionary If your copy of the extension, or your settings, point to a hosted dictionary. The extension checks it periodically. An ordinary download request for the dictionary file. As with any web request, the server that hosts it can see your IP address and browser details. No personal data is added to the request.
monday.com (api.monday.com) Only if an administrator connects a monday.com API token (board sync), or runs a bulk term import. Requests made with that token, which read board, group, column and label names, and create or update the one board where the dictionary is kept. No other board is written to.
OpenAI (api.openai.com) Only when you press “Translate with AI” and confirm. You are shown the list of terms before anything is sent. The German terms to be translated, what kind of term each one is (column, label, and so on), and the name of the board it appears on, sent with your own OpenAI API key. OpenAI’s handling of this data is governed by OpenAI’s policies and your agreement with OpenAI.
Your email program Only when you choose to send suggestions or answers to your team. The extension opens a draft email, or copies text to your clipboard, containing the terms, your suggestions and your name if you entered it. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Data sent to monday.com and OpenAI is then subject to those companies’ own privacy policies and to the agreements your organisation has with them.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or fingerprinting.
  • It does not read your browsing history or run on websites other than monday.com, apart from fetching the dictionary address described above.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for any purpose other than translating what you see on monday.com.
  • It does not use your data to assess creditworthiness or for lending.

The use of information received by the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data in local extension storage stays on your computer until you delete it. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of it. You can also remove a stored monday.com token with “Disconnect token” and an OpenAI key with the “API key…” button, both in the translation editor’s settings. Translations an administrator has saved to the monday.com dictionary board are kept in monday.com until someone deletes that board or its items.

Children

The extension is a workplace tool for monday.com users and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a new version of the extension changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the effective date above will change.
